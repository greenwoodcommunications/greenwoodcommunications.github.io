Thomas’s Top 10 2025-2026
4. Case Competitions
Business Case Competitions are so Greenwood! We once again saw record numbers of participants in these events, at both the 
Senior and Junior level. And a team of Grade 8 students won their way into the Senior Case Competition (from the Junior Case 
Competition). I love that we welcome alumni, parents and past parents to participate, making it a true community event. I often 
hear from alumni that taking part in these competitions at Greenwood set them up for future success.
3. Greenwood Community Golf Tournament
We had not hosted a community golf tournament in many, many years. And, in September 2025, it was time to bring back this 
fabulous event. Parents past and present, staff, alumni and friends of Greenwood joined us for a gorgeous day on the greens.  
The funds raised support our Athletics Programming.
