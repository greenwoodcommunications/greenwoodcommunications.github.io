“Our Outdoor Education model is a real strength. 
It’s already fairly unique in its scope, with the 
whole school out twice a year by grade, and it 
has allowed us to stay true to our OE roots while 
also looking for meaningful ways to make course 
connections on trips. Having an entire grade, 
along with many of their teachers, together for a 
week creates some pretty unique opportunities 
for connection-making. The Grade 8 Quebec City 
trip is a great example: students on this trip get a 
strong OE experience while also making tangible 
connections to their social studies and French 
courses.” – Sam Clark
