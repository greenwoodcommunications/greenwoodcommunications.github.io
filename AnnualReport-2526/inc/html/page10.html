2. Middle School Assemblies
Our Grade 7 and 8 students are such an important piece of Greenwood’s fabric. This year we started Middle School Assemblies 
to highlight this community and the leadership opportunities for Grade 7 and 8 students at Greenwood. These assemblies were 
a lovely way to celebrate our middle schoolers!

So, I began to think of the AMAZING Greenwood students and all the interesting, creative, intellectual, and leading edge

From starting new clubs like fishing by Owen S., to assembly addresses like Tessa C.’s on boredom, to our senior students
