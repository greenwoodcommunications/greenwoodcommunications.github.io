Greenwood College School Foundation
The Foundation is a separately incorporated body that manages and oversees 
endowment funds established through philanthropic giving to support Greenwood 
College School. The financial statements for the Foundation showed assets of 
$8,877,578 as of June 30, 2026. 
Greenwood’s endowment is a collection of funds that hold investments in 
perpetuity as a source of income. Each year, only a disbursement is spent. The 
disbursement is between 3.5 to 5% of the average value of the investment during 
the 24 months prior to the fiscal year. The capital remains invested and protected to 
continue growing. 
The funds are overseen and managed by the Greenwood College School 
Foundation, with the goal of ensuring the sustainability of the school for the long-
term. The following funds are held in the Greenwood College School Foundation. 
Here, we share the closing balances as of June 30, 2026.
THE GENERAL ENDOWMENT FUND | $522,979.55
Established by the proceeds from the Upward Bound Gala, the Fund supports the 
school’s areas of greatest need.

through the generosity of the Class of 2006 families, the Fund supports student
