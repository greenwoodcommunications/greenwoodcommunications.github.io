Fundraising Financials
Your Support For Greenwood College School In 2025-2026 
Our community’s generosity allows Greenwood to continually model what education can 
be, supporting key initiatives beyond what tuition alone can cover. Your gifts ensure we can 
flexibly adapt and protect the Greenwood Approach, addressing urgent needs and emerging 
opportunities in real time. During the 2025-2026 school year, your support directly enriched 
the student experience through the initiatives highlighted below. Thank you for helping us 
ensure that every student is seen, known, and championed. 
GREENWOOD FUND: The Greenwood Fund supports the school’s greatest areas of need, 
as determined by the Principal. Funds are strategically allotted in ways that resonate with 
the Greenwood community while supporting priority projects. During the 2025-2026 school 
year, the Greenwood Fund supported a wide variety of arts, athletics and experiential 
opportunities for students. 
ARTS: Our donors generously supported the arts this past year, including providing 
funding for a new theatre short-throw laser projector; new drafting tables for our 
visual arts program; high-quality percussion instruments for our music department 
and so much more.
ATHLETICS: This year, because of your generosity, we were able to purchase 
Greenwood-branded athletics chairs, bringing school spirit, professionalism and 
comfort to our sport sidelines. 
ACADEMICS: Thanks to our donors, student makers in our new “World of Fashion” 
course now work with professional sewing machines. Donor funding also brought 
advanced air filtration and thermal control to our Innovation Lab, creating a state-
of-the art environment as students bring their ideas to life on the laser printers. 
Additionally, gifts welcomed industry partners directly into our classes and 
competitions, and supported the implementation of platforms like exam.net during 
student assessments.
