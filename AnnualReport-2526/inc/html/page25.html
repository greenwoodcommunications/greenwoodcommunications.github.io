Fundraising Financials
Your Support For Greenwood College School Foundation
The Greenwood College School Foundation is a separately incorporated body that 
manages and oversees endowment funds established through your philanthropic giving to 
support Greenwood College School. During the 2025-2026 academic year, $326,327 was 
disbursed from the Foundation to support key priority areas of the school, including: 
STUDENT FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE:  Greenwood believes a transformative education 
should be defined by a student’s curiosity and character, never by their financial 
circumstances. Today, rising economic complexities mean more qualified families require 
financial assistance to join or remain in our community. In 2025-2026, we were able to 
fund a total of $230,290 for partial tuition for 11 students in Grades 7-12 through Financial 
Assistance. This includes $45,753 that was deferred from the 2024-2025 school year. 
Thanks to the generosity of our donor community, we can say yes to exceptional students 
who are ready to contribute to our classrooms right now. 
GENERAL ENDOWMENT FUND:  The General Endowment Fund directly supported 
student learning, technology-rich resources, and well-being. In 2025-2026, we were able to 
fund $79,887 across multiple school priorities, including classroom-connected experiential 
learning opportunities that brought course concepts to life in tangible ways and specialized 
applications to enhance student learning (e.g., exam.net, SoundTrap music software, and 
Google Workspace tools such as Gemini and Google Assignments). To champion both 
creative expression and physical well-being, endowment funds were also used for camera 
equipment in our film and photography programs alongside additional Fitness Room 
equipment. Finally, we funded Mental Health First Aid certification for 10 staff members, 
ensuring our team remains fully prepared to care for and support every student every day. 
•	 EXPERIENTIAL LEARNING: Through endowment funding, we can provide a lasting 
foundation for rich, hands-on education at Greenwood. In 2025-2026, $27,414 in 
funding directly supported classroom-connected experiential learning opportunities 
that brought course concepts to life and enabled students to do authentic work of the 
discipline. Donor support for endowments enabled dynamic field trips and meaningful 
partnerships with visiting experts. Local visual artists and musicians, for example, 
facilitated hands-on workshops and offered personal feedback on student creative 
work. By championing these endowment funds, our donors ensure that every student 
continues to benefit from vibrant, real-world learning that sparks curiosity and deepens 
understanding for years to come.
