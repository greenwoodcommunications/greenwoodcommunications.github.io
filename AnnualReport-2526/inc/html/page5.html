Greenwood College School Annual Report 2025–2026 |  Page 5
Dear Greenwood community,
It’s my pleasure to provide you with the 2025-
2026 Annual Report, an opportunity to share 
highlights of the school year, reflect on the 
achievements within our Venture Further 
Together 2021-2026 Strategic Plan, and 
celebrate Greenwood’s donors and volunteers for 
their contributions to our school community. 
This year marked the final year of the Venture 
Further Together strategic plan, and as we 
closed out that plan, we focused our efforts on 
strengthening what truly makes Greenwood, 
Greenwood: amazing experiential learning, 
character development in all we do, and 
community connection. We made great strides 
across all our strategic priority areas, and you 
can read more about these achievements in the 
following pages.
The 2025-2026 year saw the construction (and 
now completion!) of our Greenspace! It was 
amazing to watch the space take shape, and, 
earlier this fall, we were excited to celebrate 
the opening of this long-awaited addition to our 
campus. It has been quite a journey to bring this 
key part of our campus masterplan to fruition, 
and we are thrilled to see it ready for use. We 
look forward to seeing our students and staff 
play, gather and learn in our new space.
While we celebrate the successes of our previous 
strategic plan, much of the 2025-2026 school 
year was also dedicated to looking forward. We 
embarked on an extensive consultation process 
to develop our next strategic plan: A Different 
School of Thought. By engaging our students, 
staff, parents, alumni, and board members, 
we ensured that this new strategy is not just a 
document, but a reflection of our full community’s 
values and ambitions. This collaborative 
journey has energized us, providing a clear and 
inspiring road map as we move into the next 
decade of Greenwood’s story with a focus on 
Greenwood’s leadership in personalized learning, 
transformational and student-driven experiences, 
developing dynamic staff, and cultivating a 
vibrant community. 
To our students, staff, families, alumni, donors 
and volunteers: thank you. Your dedication, 
partnership, and spirit are what make Greenwood 
truly special. As we close this chapter and 
look toward the future, I am deeply grateful for 
your ongoing support and for everything you 
contribute to our community. The strength and 
warmth of the Greenwood community continues 
to inspire me each day.
With gratitude,
Heather Thomas
Principal, Head of School
