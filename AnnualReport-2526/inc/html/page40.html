Greenwood is committed to protecting the privacy of all members of its community. When students register at Greenwood, parent/
guardian information is shared within the school so that we can keep you informed through various publications, invite you to special 
events and involve you as volunteers and supporters. All information is kept in a confidential database and secure files. Under no 
circumstances do we share, sell or rent our student, parent/guardian and/or donor information. Access to this information is limited 
within the school to authorized persons who are required to act in accordance with our privacy standards, for school purposes only. 
For more information on our privacy policy, please contact Kelly Giannoccaro, Vice Principal, Finance and Operations.
