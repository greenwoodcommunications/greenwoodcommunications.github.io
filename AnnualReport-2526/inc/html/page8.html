6. The Greenspace!
I’ll admit I’m disappointed that weather and other factors prevented the Greenspace from opening in the 2025-2026 
academic year. As of September 2026, we are thrilled to welcome you to the new space and begin using it for our 
HPE classes and Athletics practices and as a community gathering space!
5. ART by Doug Brown
For the first time ever, Greenwood’s senior play was a piece written by a Greenwood staff member, Doug Brown! ART is delightful 
farce centring around a suburban family whose perfectly ordinary afternoon unravels the moment there’s an unexpected knock at 
the door. Congratulations to all involved. And a huge thank you to Doug for trusting your work to Greenwood.
